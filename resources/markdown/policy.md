# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 16, 2026

ConvoConnect ("**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") operates the ConvoConnect social commerce platform for WhatsApp (the "**Service**"), available at our website and related applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, use the Service, or interact with us in other ways.

Please read this policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

---

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

- **Business users** — individuals and organizations that register for and use ConvoConnect (account owners, administrators, agents, and staff).
- **Website visitors** — people who browse our marketing site, blog, booking pages, or public shop pages.
- **End customers** — individuals whose personal data is processed through the Service when our business users communicate with them via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or related channels.

When our business users upload contacts, send messages, or collect orders and bookings through ConvoConnect, those users are generally the **data controllers** for their end customers' information. ConvoConnect acts as a **data processor** on their behalf, processing that data according to their instructions and our agreement with them. End customers should contact the relevant business directly for questions about how their data is used.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide to us

When you register or use the Service, we may collect:

- **Account information** — name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, password (stored in hashed form), and billing details.
- **Business profile** — subdomain, branding, settings, team members, and role assignments.
- **Communications** — messages you send to our support team, feedback, and survey responses.
- **Payment information** — billing address and transaction records. Payment card details are processed by our payment providers (such as Stripe) and are not stored on our servers in full.

### 2.2 Information processed through the Service

When you use ConvoConnect to operate your WhatsApp business, we process data you and your customers generate, including:

- **Contact records** — names, phone numbers, email addresses, tags, custom fields, and conversation history.
- **Messages and media** — WhatsApp messages, templates, images, documents, voice notes, call metadata, and internal agent notes.
- **Commerce data** — product catalogs, orders, carts, invoices, and payment status.
- **Booking data** — appointment details, service selections, staff assignments, event registrations, and calendar sync information.
- **Campaign data** — broadcast lists, delivery and read receipts, and campaign analytics.
- **Automation data** — flow submissions, WhatsApp Flow responses, and workflow events.

### 2.3 Information collected automatically

When you access our website or Service, we may automatically collect:

- **Device and usage data** — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and timestamps.
- **Log data** — API requests, error reports, and security events.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — see Section 10 below.

### 2.4 Information from third parties

We may receive information from:

- **Meta / WhatsApp** — when you connect a WhatsApp Business account through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), including phone number verification status, message delivery events, and template approval status.
- **Payment providers** — such as Stripe or M-Pesa, to confirm transactions.
- **Integrations you enable** — such as Shopify, WooCommerce, Google Calendar, Twilio, or SMTP providers, according to the permissions you grant.
- **Authentication providers** — if you sign in using a third-party login method.
